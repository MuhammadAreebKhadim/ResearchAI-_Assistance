About

Built for researchers,
not just readers.

Research is a process — and the hardest part is often not the thinking itself, but the navigation. Finding the right papers among millions, understanding dense academic writing, and deciding who to collaborate with are real friction points that slow down progress.

ResearchAI was built to remove that friction. It searches across Semantic Scholar, ArXiv, and OpenAlex — three of the largest open research databases — giving you broad coverage across all fields.

The professor collaboration feature is particularly useful for PhD students and postdocs looking for advisors, or researchers exploring new institutional partnerships.
